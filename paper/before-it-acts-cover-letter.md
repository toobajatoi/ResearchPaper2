Dear Editor,

I am submitting “Before It Acts: A Scoping Review of Preview and Approval in Agentic AI Interfaces” as a Review Article. I am an independent researcher, so this message comes from a personal email address rather than an institutional one.

The paper asks what an interface shows a person before a generative AI agent sends a message, edits a file, or otherwise changes something, and what that person can still change or refuse. It is a scoping review of published studies. It did not recruit participants. A CHI 2026 extended abstract already reviews autonomy mechanisms in conversation with language models, and a 2026 preprint already surveys how agent permission policies are specified and enforced. This manuscript stays on the preview itself and cites those papers as boundaries.

The search was run on 1 October 2026 through public web search. Scopus, Web of Science, and IEEE Xplore were not available. Ten studies were extracted. The protocol, search log, screening log, and extraction sheet accompany the manuscript. Grok, used through Cursor, assisted with the search, screening, extraction, and draft. That assistance is disclosed in the manuscript. I am the only author.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
