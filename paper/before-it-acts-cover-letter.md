I am submitting “Before It Acts: A Scoping Review of Preview and Approval in Agentic AI Interfaces” as a Review Article. I am an independent researcher in Karachi, Pakistan. I do not have an institutional email address or an institutional profile page, so this message comes from a personal email address. My public scholarly profile is the ORCID record above.

The journal publishes research on how people interact with computing systems. This review is about that interaction at a specific moment: what an interface shows before a generative agent sends a message, edits a file, spends money, or otherwise changes something outside the conversation, and what the person can still change or refuse. The article type is a review of published studies. It did not recruit participants. The design statements are propositions from the synthesis, not a claim of a validated framework.

Fifteen studies were included. Thirty-two records were assessed, and seventeen were excluded. The searches were run on 1 October 2026. The first two passes used public web search and public ACM Digital Library and IEEE Xplore pages. The third pass recorded arXiv API hit counts for `cs.HC` (19) and `cs.CR` (111). A direct ACM Digital Library search returned a bot check and no hit count. Scopus and Web of Science were not searched. Three neighboring reviews are cited as boundaries rather than as the map this paper draws: a CHI 2026 extended abstract on autonomy mechanisms, a 2026 preprint on permission policies, and a 2026 preprint that codes how much information a runtime-approval prompt shows. What this manuscript adds is approval integrity, whether the approved object is the object that runs, and an extraction of what the person can still edit.

The protocol, search log, screening log, extraction sheet, and PRISMA-ScR checklist are deposited at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23082979. Grok 4.7, accessed through Cursor 3.17.8, assisted with the search, proposed screening and extraction, and the draft. The manuscript disclosure states which sources were checked against full text and which were checked against an abstract or publisher page. I am the only author.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
