# PRISMA-ScR checklist

Completed for “Before It Acts: A Scoping Review of Preview and Approval in Agentic AI Interfaces.”

The items follow Tricco et al. (2018), *Annals of Internal Medicine, 169*(7), 467–473, https://doi.org/10.7326/M18-0850.

The journal requires an unstructured abstract of at most 150 words, so item 2 is met in substance and not with PRISMA headings. Critical appraisal (items 12 and 16) was not done. The protocol is in this record and is not registered.
